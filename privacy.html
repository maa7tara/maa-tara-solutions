Privacy notice

Last updated 15 November 2026

This notice explains what we do with the personal details you send us when you ask for a quote, book a job or enquire about a business contract. We only use them to deal with your enquiry and any work that follows.

Who we are

Maa Tara Solutions Ltd is the controller of your personal data. We are a private limited company registered in England and Wales, company number 16567143. Our registered office is Unit 82a James Carter Road, Mildenhall, Bury St Edmunds, IP28 7DE. For anything about your data, email enquiries@maatara.co.uk or phone 07741801735.

How our forms work

Our website does not store what you type. When you press send, the form opens your own email app with the details filled in, and nothing reaches us until you send that email. The website sets no cookies and uses no tracking or advertising tools.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, job title, email address, phone number and how you prefer to be contacted.
  • Business details: company name, Companies House number, sector, business postcode and region.
  • Job details: collection and delivery postcodes, what is being moved, quantities, dates, schedules and site access notes.
  • Your choices: whether you agreed to us using your details, and whether you want occasional updates from us.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To reply, quote and plan the work. This is needed to take steps you have asked for before a contract, and to carry out that contract.
  • To keep records we must keep by law, such as invoices and accounts.
  • To send occasional updates about our capacity and services, only if you ticked the box. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Who else sees your details

  • Google, which provides our email. Your email to us is stored in our Google Workspace account.
  • Subcontracted transport operators, only when we need one to price or carry out your job, and only the details they need for it.
  • postcodes.io, a free UK postcode lookup. When you type a postcode into our business contracts form, the postcode alone is sent to it so we can check it and fill in your region. No other details are sent.
  • GitHub, which hosts this website, and Google Fonts, which supplies its lettering. Like any web host, they receive your device's internet address when you visit.
  • Our accountants, insurers or legal advisers, or public authorities, when the law requires it.

Some of these providers may handle data outside the UK. Where they do, they use the safeguards UK data protection law requires. We never sell your details.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work: up to 12 months.
  • Customers: for as long as we work together, then 6 years for tax and legal records.
  • Updates list: until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your details, correct them, delete them, limit how we use them, or object to us using them. You can also withdraw your agreement to updates at any time. Email enquiries@maatara.co.uk and we will reply within one month.

Complaints

Please tell us first so we can put things right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
